      Technical & System Design Flow

      1. Clarify & measure first: Define scope, constraints, and baseline telemetry before proposing fixes.
      2. Isolate root cause: Separate compute, database, network, or product assumptions systematically.
      3. State trade-offs: Compare at least two viable options and explain why you picked one.
      4. Verify & operate: Cover testing, rollout safety, monitoring, and rollback criteria.

      STAR Behavioral Checklist

      1. Situation (15%): Brief context and why the problem mattered to users or the team.
      2. Task (15%): Your specific ownership and constraints—avoid vague "we" everywhere.
      3. Action (50%): Concrete decisions you made, obstacles faced, and alternatives rejected.
      4. Result (20%): Quantified outcome plus one concrete lesson you carried forward.

      High-Signal Questions to Ask

      • What does a strong first 90 days look like in this role?
      • Where is the biggest technical or product bottleneck for the team right now?
      • How are engineering priorities balanced against reliability and maintenance work?
